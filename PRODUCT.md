## Platform

web

## Users
Primary decision-maker: **parents** of students preparing for the PKN STAN selection (they pay and decide which bimbel to trust). Secondary: the students themselves (grade 12 SMA/SMK/MA and gap-year alumni) who usually find the site first and forward it to a parent. Most visits come from a phone.

## Product Purpose
Bimbel Infinity (PT Infinity Global Inspiratif) prepares students for the PKN STAN entrance selection (SKD: TWK, TIU, TKP). The website must earn a parent's trust and turn it into a conversation with the admin (WhatsApp / phone) or a registration form.

## Positioning
Founded on 17 April 2013 by PKN STAN students; tutors are PKN STAN students and alumni; classes in Bintaro directly in front of the PKN STAN campus. Neighbouring bimbels cannot truthfully claim the same origin and location.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML/CSS/JS served by `server.js` (Node). Real contact details must stay real (this is a live business site). Open decisions: WhatsApp number, program prices and durations, guarantee terms, the year behind the 90% pass-rate figure, alumni names, 2019–2026 history, which cities are still active. These are shown as marked placeholders (`.ph`) and must never be invented.

## Product Principles
- Trust before persuasion: every claim on the page is verifiable (origin, location, press, dated figures).
- Parents decide: answers to cost, guarantee, schedule and who teaches must be one tap away.
- One clear next step: chat with the admin or register.
- Phone first: the page must work on a mid-range phone over mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA text contrast; keyboard-operable controls; respects reduced motion; Indonesian language (`lang="id"`).
